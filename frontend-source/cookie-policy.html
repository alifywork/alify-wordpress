What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by a website on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage or pixels, can remember preferences, support functionality, and help understand website usage.

Cookies we may use

Essential storage supports security and core functionality. Preference storage remembers choices such as dismissing an interface element. Analytics cookies may be introduced only when an analytics service is configured and, where required, consent has been obtained.

Current interface storage

The current website uses session storage to remember dismissal of the mobile project prompt for the browsing session. This is functional interface storage rather than advertising tracking.

Managing your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may affect website functionality. Browser help pages explain how to review stored data and change permissions.

Third-party cookies

Embedded content, analytics, fonts, or external services may set their own cookies if added or activated. Their providers’ notices explain the relevant purposes and controls.
