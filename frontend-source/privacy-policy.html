Information we collect

We may collect information you choose to provide through inquiries, such as your name, email address, company, project details, and any links or materials you share. Basic technical and usage information may also be processed by hosting, security, or analytics providers when those services are enabled.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, assess and deliver requested services, maintain website security, understand website performance, meet legal obligations, and protect our legitimate business interests. We do not sell personal information.

Legal bases and retention

Where applicable, processing relies on consent, steps requested before entering a contract, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, or legal obligations. Information is retained only as long as needed for those purposes and applicable record-keeping requirements.

Sharing and processors

Limited information may be shared with service providers supporting hosting, email, analytics, security, or professional advice. Providers receive only the information needed for their role and are subject to appropriate contractual or legal safeguards.

Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or export personal information, and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to a relevant data protection authority.
